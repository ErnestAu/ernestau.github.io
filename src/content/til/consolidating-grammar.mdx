## early learning experience

i was tackling about 0-3 new grammar points per day. in the beginning, it was pretty doable.

but as the grammar points piled up, i started conflating a lot of them together.

for example. 'to eat' in jp is 食べる. i would think ok, so if i wanna add politeness, it's obviously 食べです. to which i would find out oh for actions the polite particle is ます, so ok it's 食べます. ok well what if i wanna do it in past tense? i know you add 'た' to make it past tense. sooo it should be 食べますた. and then it's like noooo absolutely not. it's 食べました. for the polite particle it's different. alright alright. well what about the casual form? 食べる ends with a る so it should be 食べった right? of course not! it only does if the furigana before る ends with 'a', 'u', or 'o' (and even then, there are exceptions). since it doesn't end with any of that, it's just 食べた. and just when i finally think i got it, the て form is lurking in the corner.
